## Lessons for mission authoring

1. Verify the running revision after loading. DCS accepted a mission-load request
   while the old revision remained active. A subsequent restart loaded revision 2;
   a request acknowledgement alone must never be treated as successful replacement.
2. Check whole footprints and final unit positions. Some candidate centres were dry
   and locally flat while their surrounding footprint crossed water. The selected
   placements were checked for intended surface and slope; these samples do not
   establish collision clearance for every scenery object.
3. Allow time for AI traffic. The initially stationary taxi test subsequently took
   off and recovered; the early observation did not establish a permanent taxi bug.
4. Separate initialization from behaviour. Adding EWR established IADS membership,
   not an engagement fix. Audio trigger flags establish execution, not audibility.
